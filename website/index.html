Built to know as little as possible

The parts that make a call work, and nothing else.

End-to-end encrypted

Audio and video go straight between the two devices, encrypted. A safety number lets you check that nobody is in between.

Clients join with a link

Send a link. Your client opens it in any browser, checks camera and microphone, and waits until you let them in.

Nothing stored on servers

No accounts, no database. The server only connects calls, in memory. Contacts and history stay on your device, encrypted.

Hide your IP address

Turn it on and calls go through the relay, so the other person never sees where you are connecting from.

Holds on weak networks

Calls reconnect when the network changes, adapt to a slow connection, and drop to voice rather than freeze.

Your own server, if you like

Organisations can run Sotto on their own server with one command, and point the apps at it.
